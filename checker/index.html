How scoring works
  • Red — your domain is open to spoofing right now: SPF is missing or broken, or DMARC is missing. Attackers can send mail as you with no policy to stop them.
  • Yellow — partial protection. DMARC exists but only monitors (p=none) or quarantines (p=quarantine); SPF uses softfail (~all) so fakes still slip through; only one DKIM selector is published; or MTA-STS is missing so your mail can travel unencrypted.
  • Green — hardened: SPF with hardfail (-all) or neutral, DMARC at p=reject, DKIM published, MX present, and MTA-STS present. An overall green means every check passed — one weak link still flags.
