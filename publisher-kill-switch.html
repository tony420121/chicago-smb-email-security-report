Microsoft just turned off Publisher — the subscription version won't open anymore.

October 1, 2026: the kill switch fired. Publisher 365 won't launch at all — not end-of-support, just off. Perpetual Office 2021 Publisher still works, but goes unsupported October 13. Here's where your .pub files go.

What happened

On October 1, 2026, Microsoft pulled the kill switch on Publisher 365: existing installs stop opening entirely, and new installs won't include it. This is a kill switch, not end-of-support — there's no patch to wait for, no grace period to exploit, no workaround where the app "mostly works." (Reported by Office Watch, TheStreet, and Computerworld.)

Which version do you have — the 60-second check

  • Publisher 365 (the subscription one): it stopped opening on October 1, 2026. If Publisher on your machine is the subscription version, it's already dead — the app won't launch.
  • Perpetual Office 2021 / 2019 Publisher: still opens today, but loses security fixes on October 13, 2026 — twelve days from the kill.
  • Open any Office app → Account → see whether your subscription includes Microsoft 365 apps or a perpetual Office version. That's the check.

Your .pub files don't die with the app — but nothing new will open them properly, so convert them now, while you still have a working app to convert with:

  • Save As / Export to Word — for text-heavy layouts (newsletters, letters). Formatting will shift; budget a cleanup pass.
  • Export to PowerPoint — for page-layout documents where positioning matters more than text flow.
  • Export / Print to PDF — for anything you only need to read or archive going forward. PDF freezes the layout as-is.

Why your shop should care — the honest version

Worth saying plainly: coverage of the exact kill date conflicted across outlets, so this date was held back until it was confirmed across Office Watch, TheStreet, and Computerworld — October 1, 2026 is the verified date.

Also the uncomfortable part: Publisher has been a dying product for years, and most shops have been phasing it out informally for a long time. The shock isn't "Publisher is gone" — it's that the subscription version stopped opening, which breaks any machine where it was still quietly doing a job nobody tracked. That's the machine that hurts you: the one with the forgotten .pub workflow nobody converted because "it's still working."

Three actions — do these this month

1. Identify every machine with Publisher 365

Those installs are dead as of October 1 — confirm nothing business-critical still needs them. Check for the subscription version on every machine, not just the ones people remember using it.

2. Convert the .pub files — before October 13

Word, PowerPoint, PDF — pick the format per document and do it while you still have a working app to convert with. After October 13 the perpetual version goes unsupported too, and the conversion job gets harder.

3. Know the whole October 13 list

Publisher 2021's support death is one card on a bigger table — Windows 11 24H2 and Office 2021 updates all end the same day. Read the October 13 convergence for the full picture.
